Agent Skill: build-real-estate

Automated execution workflow for luxury real estate and architectural showcases.

💡 What does invoking this skill do?

When you invoke @build-real-estate, the AI agent builds a real estate development portal with filterable apartment types, pop-up architectural floor plan modals, image carousels, and appointment booking drawers.

Workflow Phases

  1. Inventory Population: Maps floor plans, sqft, and prices into src/data/units.ts.
  2. Blueprint Lightbox: Integrates Radix dialog for zooming into architectural blueprints.
  3. Amenities & Carousel: Embeds Embla Carousel for swiping through luxury photos.
  4. Appointment Capture: Configures pinned button opening a private viewing booking drawer.
