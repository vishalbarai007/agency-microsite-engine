Agent Skill: build-static-landing

Automated execution workflow for single-page conversion landing sites.

💡 What does invoking this skill do?

When you trigger @build-static-landing in Antigravity or Claude, the AI reads your client-spec.json and builds a complete, responsive 5-section landing page with working Google Sheet forms in minutes.

Workflow Phases

  1. Intake Parse: Reads client brand name, tagline, and chosen vibe.
  2. Token Application: Configures typography and Tailwind palettes.
  3. Content Segregation: Populates pure data files in src/data/.
  4. Page Assembly: Combines Navbar, Hero, FeatureGrid, ContactForm, and Footer.
