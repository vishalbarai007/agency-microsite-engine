Micro-Ecommerce & WhatsApp Checkout

High-converting boutique merchandise sales with Zustand persistent cart and dual WhatsApp/Stripe checkout routing.

WhatsApp Direct Order Zustand Cart Store Stripe Checkout Option Zero Monthly SaaS Fees
💡 In Plain English (Why small shops love this)

Setting up a full Shopify or WooCommerce store takes weeks and costs hundreds of dollars in monthly fees. For a brand launching a limited batch of 3 products (like a signature perfume, merch hoodie, or luxury coffee roast):

  • Visitors can click "Add to Bag" and see an instant slide-out cart drawer.
  • Items stay saved in their cart even if they refresh or close the tab.
  • One-Click WhatsApp Order: Clicking checkout opens WhatsApp with their entire cart neatly itemized and priced, ready to send with one tap.
  • Stripe Card Payments: Or, if the client prefers credit card processing, it automatically forwards to Stripe.

🚀 Key Architectural Perks

  • Zustand: Lightning fast state without bulky context providers or unnecessary page re-renders.
  • Local Persistence: Automatically remembers user items using HTML5 localStorage.
  • Zero Abandoned Cart Fees: Direct conversational sales via WhatsApp close up to 3x higher than standard card checkout in many regions.
