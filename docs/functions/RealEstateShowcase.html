⚡ The 2-Day Agency Delivery Framework

Deliver enterprise-grade luxury landing pages with 80%+ profit margins by adhering to the strictly decoupled 2-day workflow:

🗓️ Day 1: Scaffold V1 & Staging Preview
  • Extract dynamic theme colors directly from company/project logo.
  • Configure project spec & RERA details in client-spec.json.
  • Populate decoupled contracts: src/data/units.ts, amenities.ts, projects.ts.
  • Scaffold 10 core sections + 3 persistent interactive modals.
  • Deploy live V1 staging preview to Vercel/Netlify for client review.
🚀 Day 2: Revisions, QA & Final Launch
  • Apply client feedback strictly via src/data/*.ts (zero JSX alterations).
  • Verify Google Sheets spreadsheet webhook & Resend email lead dispatch.
  • Audit smooth scroll (Lenis/GSAP), image zoom, and mobile responsiveness.
  • Ensure MahaRERA / RERA certificate & QR code compliance.
  • Connect custom client domain and hand over production build.

🎨 Aesthetic Principles & Logo-Derived Theming

1. Minimal Content, Rich Imagery Rule (75/25 Ratio): Limit text to punchy editorial statements and metadata badges. Let the architecture, dusk lighting, and macro material textures (Italian marble, raw stone, bronze) do the selling.

2. Editorial Typography & Italic Accents: Pair a luxury serif heading (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display) with modern sans body (Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter). Style 1-2 emotive words per headline in an elegant, lighter italic serif.

3. Company Logo Theming: Derive primary and secondary accents from the client's provided logo (e.g. Sapphire Blue, Tuscan Terracotta, Gold/Noir, or Minimalist Blanc & Noir) while retaining universal luxury denominators: hairline borders, image hover zoom, and smooth inertia scroll.
