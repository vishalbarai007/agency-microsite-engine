💡 In Plain English (What does this do?)

When someone visits the client's microsite and fills out the contact form, this system makes sure:

  • The visitor typed a real email and didn't leave critical fields blank (checked immediately in their browser).
  • Spam bots are automatically caught using an invisible trap field ("honeypot") without annoying humans with CAPTCHAs.
  • Their inquiry instantly appears as a new row in the client's Google Sheet so the sales team can track it.
  • The client gets an instant email alert on their phone with the full message.
  • The visitor sees a modern floating success alert ("Message received!") in under 1 second.

🛡️ Anti-Spam Honeypot: How It Works

Traditional CAPTCHAs (clicking fire hydrants or crosswalks) kill conversion rates by frustrating legitimate users. Instead, we use an invisible honeypot trap:

  • We add an extra text field named honeypot with CSS: opacity: 0; position: absolute; pointer-events: none; tab-index: -1.
  • Human users cannot see, click, or tab into this field, so it always remains blank.
  • Automated spam scrapers read raw HTML and automatically populate every input field with spam text.
  • When the server detects any text in honeypot, it returns a fake success message to the bot but never writes to the Google Sheet or sends an email.
