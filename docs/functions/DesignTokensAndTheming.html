💡 In Plain English (Why do we need design tokens?)

When you ask basic AI to "make a website", it usually makes a boring generic blue page with standard fonts that screams "AI-made". Our design token engine fixes this:

  • You tell the AI one word: "premium", "funky", "minimal", or "bold-tech".
  • The engine automatically loads matching font pairs, calculated color palettes, and container padding.
  • A luxury architectural firm instantly gets elegant serif fonts and champagne gold borders.
  • A streetwear fashion drop instantly gets thick black borders, neo-brutalist shadows, and neon colors.
