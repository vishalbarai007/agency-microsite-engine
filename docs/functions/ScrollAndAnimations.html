💡 In Plain English (Why do high-end sites need this?)

Cheap websites have jerky, mechanical scrolling. Luxury agency websites (like Apple, Tesla, or high-end architectural studios) feel buttery smooth, heavy, and expensive:

  • Lenis Momentum: When the user scrolls their mouse wheel, the page glides smoothly like floating on silk.
  • Pinned Storytelling: As you scroll down, the screen locks in place while cards glide horizontally across the screen like slides in a theater.
  • Parallax Depth: Background photos move at a slightly slower speed than text, giving a rich 3D feeling.
  • Smart Mobile Fallback: On mobile phones, heavy pinned effects automatically turn off so the phone battery doesn't drain and touch scrolling stays lightning fast.

✨ Cinematic Effects Included in the Engine

  • SmoothScrollProvider: Glues Lenis smooth scrolling to GSAP's 60fps clock with zero stutter.
  • ParallaxImage Wrapper: Gives any photo gentle optical depth based on user scroll velocity.
  • HorizontalNarrative: Converts vertical scroll wheel ticks into horizontal slide transitions on desktop screens.
  • Staggered Text Reveals: Headlines animate into view line-by-line as they enter the screen.
