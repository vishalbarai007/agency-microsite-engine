The 7-Day Sprint Playbook

The standard operating procedure (SOP) to deliver client microsites in 5 to 7 days with 80%+ agency profit margins.

Agency SOP 80% Margin Zero Scope Creep Monthly Retainer Funnel
💡 In Plain English (Why a 7-day model?)

Most agencies lose money because clients take months to reply and ask for endless changes. The 7-Day Sprint solves this permanently:

  • Clients pay a flat fee upfront for an Express 7-Day Launch.
  • Speed becomes the key selling feature, commanding higher fees.
  • We enforce a strict single round of revisions on Day 5.
  • Websites launch on time, the client is thrilled, and the agency transitions them into a $99/month care retainer.

📅 The 7-Day Production Timeline

Day 1 Time spent: ~45 mins
Intake & Repository Setup

Client completes the 6-question form. Developer clones the starter template, pastes responses into client-spec.json, and creates the Google Sheet webhook.

Day 2 Time spent: 1 - 2 hours
Autonomous AI Codegen

Trigger Antigravity or Claude with the matching archetype skill. The agent builds the full 5-section site, populates src/data/, and configures form actions.

Day 3 - 4 Time spent: 2 - 3 hours
Developer Polish & QA

Swap placeholder logos with client SVGs, adjust mobile typography on 375px/768px viewports, and test that form submissions write to Google Sheets and fire email alerts.

Day 5 Time spent: 30 mins
Staging Preview & 1 Unified Revision

Deploy to a temporary Vercel preview URL. The client reviews on their phone and provides their single consolidated list of text/photo adjustments.

Day 6 - 7 Time spent: 1 hour
Custom Domain DNS & Launch

Apply client edits to src/data/, map custom domain DNS records, verify SSL certificate, hand over the project, and pitch the monthly maintenance retainer.
