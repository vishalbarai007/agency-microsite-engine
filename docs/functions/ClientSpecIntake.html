💡 In Plain English (How to build sites with minimal client input)

Clients are notorious for delaying projects by weeks because they don't have text or photos ready. With our intake engine, you never wait for them:

  • You give the client a simple 6-question Google Form.
  • You paste their 6 answers into client-spec.json.
  • The AI agent automatically generates polished marketing headlines, selects matching icons, and sources high-definition architecture/lifestyle photos from Unsplash.
  • Within 2 hours, the client has a full working website ready to view on their phone.

📋 The 6 Client Intake Questions

# Question Prompt What the AI does with this info
Q1 Company Name & Industry Sets site title, metadata, copyright, and SEO tags.
Q2 Aesthetic Vibe (Premium, Funky, Clean, Tech) Picks the exact Google Font pairs, color schemes, and button styles.
Q3 One-Sentence Tagline Expands this into a high-impact Hero headline and subhead.
Q4 3 Core Offerings / Services Builds the 3-card service grid with Lucide icons and feature bullets.
Q5 Primary CTA Button Target Configures hero buttons (e.g. Book a Call, View Residences).
Q6 Lead Destination (Email & Google Sheet) Wires up the contact form server action.
